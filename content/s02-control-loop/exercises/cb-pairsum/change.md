The input may now have an optional third part after a second `|`, the word `distinct`, for example `4,4|8|distinct`. When it is present, the two numbers that add up to the target must also be different values, so `4,4|8|distinct` prints `false` (the two 4s are the same value), while `2,2,6|8|distinct` prints `true` (2 and 6). Without the third part, nothing changes: `4,4|8` still prints `true`.
