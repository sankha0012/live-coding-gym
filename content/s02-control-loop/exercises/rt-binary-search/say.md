I keep a window from lo to hi and look at the middle. If the middle value is smaller than the target, the answer is to the right, so lo moves up. Otherwise I move hi down to mid, which keeps equal values in the window. When the window closes, lo is the first position that is not smaller than the target, so I check it. It is O(log n) time and O(1) space. The check at the end covers a missing target and an empty list.
