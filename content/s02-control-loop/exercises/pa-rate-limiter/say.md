The model prompt fixes the algorithm details that people get wrong: the bucket starts full, refill is lazy, and tokens are capped at capacity. It makes the clock a parameter, which is the key move because it makes the limiter deterministic to test without real sleeping. It covers invalid input, a backwards clock and thread safety, which are the production concerns. A weak prompt such as "write a rate limiter" would get a fixed window or a sleep-based version that cannot be tested and has no clear refill rule.
