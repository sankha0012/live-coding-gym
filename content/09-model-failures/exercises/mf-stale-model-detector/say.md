I take the clock as a parameter so the check is deterministic and easy to test. I compute both ages in fractional days with total_seconds and compare them strictly against the limits. Using .days would truncate 7.5 days to 7 and miss a stale model. A timestamp in the future means a broken clock or bad metadata, so I raise instead of reporting a negative age. It is O(1). Checking data age as well as model age matters because a freshly retrained model on old data is still stale.
