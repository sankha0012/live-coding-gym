The bug is that replace(tzinfo=...) only attaches a label to the wall-clock time and does not convert the instant. A UTC timestamp at 02:30 became 02:30 in Sao Paulo, which is the wrong day for events near midnight. I spot it by testing timestamps just before and after local midnight. The fix is to treat a naive timestamp as UTC with replace, and then call astimezone to convert into the target zone. Using zoneinfo rather than a fixed offset also handles daylight saving. The cost is still O(n).
