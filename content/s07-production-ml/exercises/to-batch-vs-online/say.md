"Batch inference scores many records at once on a schedule, for example every night, and saves the predictions in a table. Online inference scores one request the moment it arrives, usually behind an API with a latency limit of tens or hundreds of milliseconds. The deciding question is how fresh the input must be: if the answer can be a few hours old, batch is simpler and cheaper. For example, I would precompute daily restaurant recommendations in batch, but check a payment for fraud online, because the decision depends on this transaction right now. The price of online is more infrastructure, monitoring and the risk that features at serving time differ from training. The price of batch is stale predictions and wasted compute on users who never come back, so many teams combine both."
