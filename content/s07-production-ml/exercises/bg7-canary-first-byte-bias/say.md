The assignment is stable and uses hashlib, so it looks right, but the bucket is computed from the first byte of the digest only. That byte has 256 possible values, and 256 does not divide evenly by 100, so the first 56 buckets are hit three times out of 256 and the rest two times. At 5 percent that sends about 5.9 percent of users to the canary, and at 50 percent about 59 percent. The fix is to use the whole digest as an integer, `int(digest.hexdigest(), 16) % 100`, where the leftover bias is negligible. I confirmed it by running many ids and checking the share. The lesson is that a stable hash is not enough; the way you reduce it to a bucket must also be uniform.
