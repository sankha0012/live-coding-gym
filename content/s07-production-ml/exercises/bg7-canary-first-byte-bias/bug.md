The assistant took only the first byte of the digest (`digest[0] % 100`). A byte has 256 values and 256 is not a multiple of 100, so buckets 0 to 55 each get three byte values and buckets 56 to 99 only two. The low buckets are over-represented, so a 5 percent canary actually receives about 5.9 percent of users, and the A/B or canary comparison no longer matches the traffic share you planned.
