I run both models on every input and keep a case when the absolute score difference is above the tolerance. The shadow call is wrapped in try/except and counted separately, because a broken shadow must not crash serving and should not be mixed into the disagreement rate. The rate divides by the inputs that were actually compared, and I guard the empty case. Sorting the disagreements by diff gives the worst cases for a human to inspect. It is O(n log n) because of the sort, and the live model still gets to raise, since a live failure is a real incident.
