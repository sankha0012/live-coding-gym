A ColumnTransformer applies a different transformer to each group of columns and joins the results side by side. Numbers get a StandardScaler, text categories get a one-hot encoder. I set handle_unknown to ignore because in production a new category will appear, and I want a row of zeros, not a crash. Columns I do not list are dropped by default, which keeps stray ids out of the model. It is linear in rows and columns. The trade-off is that one-hot grows wide for high-cardinality columns.
