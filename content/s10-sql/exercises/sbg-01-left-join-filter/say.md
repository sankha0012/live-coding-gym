The query joins only delivered orders, because the status condition sits in the ON clause. A customer who has only cancelled orders therefore gets no match and passes the IS NULL test, even though the question was about customers with no order of any status. The fix is to drop the status condition so the LEFT JOIN matches every order, and keep the NULL test in WHERE. I checked it by finding a Curitiba customer with a cancelled order and confirming they were in the wrong result and not in the right one.
