I join orders to restaurants, sum revenue per city and month in a CTE, and use LAG partitioned by city to bring the previous month of the same city onto the row. Without the partition, the first month of a city would compare against the last month of another city. Growth is the difference divided by the previous value, times 100. A city's first month has no previous row, so LAG gives NULL and so does the result. In production I would guard against a previous value of zero. Cost is one group and one sort.
