I merge orders with restaurants to get city and cuisine, then group by both columns with named aggregations. For the order count I use size, which counts rows, because count on subtotal would skip the orders with a missing subtotal. The mean already ignores NaN. Then I round, sort by count descending with city and cuisine as tie breakers so the result is deterministic, and reset the index. It is one pass for the merge and one for the grouping. The inner merge drops orders of unknown restaurants, which the task says to ignore.
