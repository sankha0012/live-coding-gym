I keep delivered orders, mark has_coupon with notna, group by it and take the mean subtotal, so I get one number with a coupon and one without. This is only a correlation. Coupons are not given at random: a code like BEMVINDO10 goes to new customers, so the groups differ in other ways. To measure the effect I would run an A/B test with random assignment, or at least compare customers with similar tenure. I would also look at the median and the counts, not only the mean.
