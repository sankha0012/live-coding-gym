For a model that predicts the future, a random split leaks information from later orders into training, so I split by time. I parse created_at to datetime, build one mask for "before the cutoff", and use the mask for train and its negation for test. One mask means no overlap and no lost rows, even for an order exactly at the cutoff. It is O(n). I would also check that the max train time is below the min test time, and ask how much of the data lands in test.
