`fix_types` gets an optional second parameter `na_values`, a tuple of strings that defaults to `()`. A `coupon_code` whose text, after stripping spaces, equals one of the strings in `na_values` also becomes NaN, in addition to the empty and spaces-only codes. The comparison is exact and case-sensitive. For example, with `fix_types(raw, na_values=("N/A", "NULL"))` the codes `"N/A"` and `" NULL "` become NaN, while `"null"` and `"SAVE10"` stay as they are. Calling `fix_types(raw)` behaves exactly as before.
