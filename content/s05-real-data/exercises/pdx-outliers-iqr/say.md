I take Q1 and Q3 of the subtotal, compute the IQR as the difference, and flag values below Q1 minus 1.5 IQR or above Q3 plus 1.5 IQR. I use the IQR because quartiles are not pulled by the outliers themselves, unlike the mean and standard deviation. Missing values compare as False, so they are never flagged, and I say that out loud. It is O(n log n) for the quantiles. I flag, I do not delete: whether a big order is an error or a real customer is a business decision.
