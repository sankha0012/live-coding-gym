I keep delivered orders, merge the city, and make two helper columns: a late flag, and minutes late clipped at zero. Then one groupby with named aggregation gives the count, the mean of the flag as the late rate, and the mean of minutes late over late rows only. These are two separate questions: how often it is late, and how late it is when it is late. If I averaged all rows the early orders would pull the number down. It is one pass over the data, O(n).
