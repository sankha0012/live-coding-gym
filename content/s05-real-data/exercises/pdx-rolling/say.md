I turn created_at into a date, count orders per day, and reindex to every calendar day so quiet days are 0. That step matters: a rolling window of 7 rows over a series with gaps would cover more than 7 days. Then I use rolling(7, min_periods=1).sum(), so the first days sum what exists and nothing is NaN. It is O(n) after the count. A time-based window like rolling("7D") gives the same result on a complete daily index.
