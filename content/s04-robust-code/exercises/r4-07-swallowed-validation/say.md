In production a swallowed validation error turns a broken upstream feed, a changed format or a locale issue into a quietly wrong metric or training set: nothing crashes, nothing alerts, and the problem is found weeks later in a business report. The strict version keeps the bad rows, measures how many there were and fails loudly when the share exceeds a tolerance; a small, known amount of dirt is accepted, a large amount is an incident. Its error message includes the count and examples, which makes the failure diagnosable.
