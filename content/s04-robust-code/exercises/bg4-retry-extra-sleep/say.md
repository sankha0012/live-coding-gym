The bug is that the function sleeps after the last failed attempt. The loop catches the error, sleeps, and only afterwards the loop ends and the error is raised, so a call that is going to fail anyway waits one extra backoff first. The fix is to sleep only when another attempt follows: skip the sleep when the attempt number is already the last, and re-raise at once. With three retries the delays should be 1, 2 and 4 seconds, not 1, 2, 4 and 8. This matters in production because the wasted wait counts against the caller's timeout.
