The bug is that the guard tests for NaN but not for infinity. `math.isnan(float('inf'))` is false, so positive and negative infinity are counted as good scores, kept in the output, and left out of the bad count. The fix is to use `math.isfinite`, which rejects NaN, inf and -inf in one call, and keep the type check in front of it so strings and bools are still rejected first. I would keep a test with all of NaN, inf and -inf, because a model that overflows produces infinity just as easily as NaN.
