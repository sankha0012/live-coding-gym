The bug is that F1 is the arithmetic mean of precision and recall instead of the harmonic mean. The harmonic mean is pulled toward the smaller of the two numbers, so a model with recall 1.0 and precision 0.01 gets an F1 near 0.02. The arithmetic mean gives it about 0.5 and hides the problem. The fix is `2 * p * r / (p + r)`, guarded so that a zero denominator returns 0.0. I would keep a test where one of the two values is very low, because that is the case where the two formulas disagree most.
