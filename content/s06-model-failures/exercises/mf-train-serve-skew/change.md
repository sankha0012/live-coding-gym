Give `serve_features` an optional third parameter `clip=None`. When `clip` is a number, the returned `amount_z` is limited to the range from `-clip` to `+clip`, so one extreme amount cannot push the model far outside what it saw in training. The `category` is unchanged. With `stats = {"mean": 2.0, "std": 1.5}`, `serve_features({"amount": 1000000.0, "category": "x"}, stats, clip=5)` returns an `amount_z` of `5.0`, and an `amount_z` of `-9.0` with `clip=5` becomes `-5.0`. Without `clip` (or with `clip=None`) nothing changes. `train_features` stays untouched.
