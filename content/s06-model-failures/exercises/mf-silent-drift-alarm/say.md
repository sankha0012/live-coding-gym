I compute the reference mean and population std once, and use k times that std as the limit. I alarm on the mean if the live mean is further than the limit from the reference mean, and on the std if the live std is. Both checks are O(n) over the two windows. Empty windows return a no_data result instead of raising, because a monitor that crashes is worse than one that stays quiet for a moment. The trade-off is that a mean-and-std check cannot see a change in shape, for example a new category mix.
