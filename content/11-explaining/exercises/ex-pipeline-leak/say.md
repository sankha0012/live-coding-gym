This script builds synthetic data, scales the features, splits into train and test, fits a logistic regression and returns the test ROC AUC. The split is stratified to keep the class ratio, and random_state makes it reproducible. Training costs about O(n times features times iterations). The subtle risk is data leakage: the scaler is fit on all rows before the split, so the mean and standard deviation include the test rows. With plain scaling the effect is small, but with imputers, target encoding or feature selection it can inflate the score a lot, and production would look worse than the report. The fix is to split first and fit the scaler on the training part only, or to put scaler and model in one sklearn Pipeline. I would also average the score over several splits, because one split is noisy.
