Worked sample: "My main area to improve is writing code from a blank file without an assistant. I learned to code with AI tools from the start, so I am strong at designing the solution, choosing metrics and reviewing results, and slower at typing code from memory. For the last few weeks I have practised every day with small timed exercises in Python and SQL, writing by hand first and explaining the code out loud. My first-try pass rate has gone from about one exercise in four to about three in four, and I can now write the common patterns, like grouping, joins and retries, without help. Meanwhile I protect the quality by writing a small test first and reading every line before I submit it."

Why it works: one real weakness, said plainly, with evidence of progress and a safeguard. Do not use "I work too hard" or "I am a perfectionist". Interviewers hear those every day and they sound evasive.
