This code splits data by time instead of at random, because in production a model always predicts the future from the past. Rows are sorted by timestamp, anything at or after the cutoff goes to test, and anything older goes to train. The gap_days option drops rows just before the cutoff. The cost is O(n log n) for the sort and O(n) memory for the two lists. The risk is label leakage through delay. If a label becomes known three days after the event, a row from two days before the cutoff has a label that was not known at the cutoff, so training on it uses information from the test period. The gap has to be at least the label delay, and I have to know that delay, so I would check it from the data. Also the cutoff is one split, so I would repeat it with several cutoffs to see if the score is stable, and I would check that both sides have enough rows.
