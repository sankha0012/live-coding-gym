# Thinking-aloud phrases


<p class="lede">Lines that keep the interviewer with you while you think.</p>
<div class="stack">
<div class="say">Let me check I understood the problem: …</div>
<div class="say">Before coding, I'll write the steps as comments.</div>
<div class="say">I'll test with a small example first.</div>
<div class="say">Here's an edge case: what if the list is empty, or the value is null?</div>
<div class="say">The complexity is O(n log n) because of the sort.</div>
<div class="say">This is correlation, not necessarily causation, because the coupons are targeted.</div>
<div class="say">If I had more time, I would…</div>
<div class="say">For the business, this means that…</div></div>
