The bug is data leakage. The scaler is fit on all rows before the split, so the test rows influence the mean and scale used on the training data, and the test score looks better than reality. The smallest fix is to split first, call `fit_transform` on the train rows only, and call `transform` on the test rows. The habit: ask "what did each step see?" for every fit. Fix the seed through `random_state` so the split is reproducible. The same rule applies to imputers, encoders and feature selectors.
