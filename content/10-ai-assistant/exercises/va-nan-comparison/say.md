The bug is `v != float("nan")`. NaN is not equal to anything, itself included, so this test is always true and NaN values are never removed; the sum becomes NaN. The fix is `math.isnan(v)`. I also keep `v is not None` as a separate check, and I do not use `if v` because that would drop valid zeros. The habit: never compare to NaN with `==`, and test with a NaN, a None, a zero and an all-invalid list. It is O(n).
