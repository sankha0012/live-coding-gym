The bug is `replace(tzinfo=utc)` on every value. For a naive value that is right, but for an aware value in another zone it keeps the wall-clock numbers and just relabels them as UTC, so the instant is wrong by the offset. The fix is a helper: if tzinfo is None, attach UTC; otherwise call `astimezone(timezone.utc)`. I do not call `astimezone` on naive values, because Python then assumes the machine's local zone and results change between servers. The habit: store and compare in UTC, and test with an aware non-UTC value.
