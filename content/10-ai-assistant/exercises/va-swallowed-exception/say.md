The bug is a bare `except` that returns 0. It hides every failure, so a corrupt value like "abc" silently becomes "zero items" and flows into the model or the bill. The fix handles the two legitimate cases, None and blank, with an explicit check, and lets `int()` raise `ValueError` for real bad data. The habit: in AI code, search for bare `except` and `except Exception` with a default return, and ask what failure it is hiding. A loud error is cheaper than a quiet wrong number.
